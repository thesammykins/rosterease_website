Choose Shifts for rostered work at a workplace. You'll build the plan here without needing an account.

Add the workplace name and, if it's useful, a location. Check the time zone too, especially if you work across different zones.

Open a shift type to check its start and end times. An end time earlier than the start means it finishes the next day.

Now build the repeating pattern. Tap a day to cycle through your shift types. Here we're changing the first day to Off. Check the pattern's start date before continuing.

Read the review, then finish setup. Check Calendar and Today against your original roster before relying on the plan. A one-off change later belongs in Calendar, not in this repeating pattern.
