If you're helping investigate a problem, open Settings, then About, and choose Local performance reports.

This is off until you choose to keep reports. Turning it on keeps a small summary on this device. It doesn't upload anything automatically.

Reports may take a day or a later launch to arrive, so an empty list doesn't mean it's broken.

When there's something to share, unlock and review the JSON first. Read what's there before sharing it. Turning the setting off deletes the local reports, not copies you've already shared.
