If you want to check what RosterEase keeps, open Settings, then Privacy. Tap How Your Records Work.

Your notes and visits stay on this phone unless you share them through another feature. Editing a saved note keeps the earlier version.

A check-in records the device's time. It doesn't independently prove attendance, and a signature doesn't verify someone's identity.

Backups are encrypted, so keep your backup key somewhere safe. For the detail on photos, recordings and signatures, use the written guide below.
