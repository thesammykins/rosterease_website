In Clients, open the more menu and choose Import clients. The app may first ask you to review the requirements for the information you'll keep. Read that before continuing.

This example uses pasted text. Tap Analyze and check each proposed client, address, duration and appointment window against the original.

Check the visit days as well. A visit is only planned on a day that's both a client visit day and one of your work days.

Apply the import when the details are right, then read the result and check the clients you've saved. An import is a starting plan for you to review, not confirmation of an appointment.
