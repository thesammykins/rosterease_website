If you want to keep check-in times, open Settings, then Notifications, and turn on Shift check-ins.

Go back to Today and find the shift you're starting. Tap Check in when you begin. That saves the time from this device.

When you finish, tap Check out. Checking out records a time, but it doesn't mark the shift as worked. Make that attendance choice on Today separately.

If a time needs correcting, open the shift's notes and check-in record. Keep the time you actually worked, rather than leaving a mistaken tap as the record.
