With Shift Plus, location review can suggest worked times for you to check. It doesn't mark a shift worked by itself.

Open Settings, then Privacy, and find Location Review. Turn it on, then check the access status underneath. Your workplace also needs the right map pin.

Allow the location access the app asks for if you want it to notice arrivals and departures in the background.

This recording shows the setup, not a real journey. When a suggestion appears on Today, check the times before confirming it. If the suggestion is wrong, edit or dismiss it.
