To follow your day without opening the app each time, open Settings, then Notifications. Turn on Show Live Activities.

If they don't appear, check that iOS allows Live Activities for RosterEase as well.

Open RosterEase when your work day starts. The activity can show your shift, or your progress through visits. It may not appear on a day off.

For a long day, reopen the app to refresh it. And if you don't want the details visible, hide Live Activity details in Privacy.
