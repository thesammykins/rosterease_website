To keep a copy of your records, open Settings, then Data Management. Tap Save Backup.

Choose where to keep the file in Files and save it. Wait for Backup Saved before you leave.

The copy is encrypted. It includes your records, note history and attachments, but not device-only choices such as the privacy lock.

Keep your backup key somewhere safe as well. Without the key used for that file, you can't open it. RosterEase doesn't keep a server copy of your backup or key.

Weekly backups are a separate option on this screen.
