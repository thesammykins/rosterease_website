Before turning on the privacy lock, make sure your device has a passcode. Open Settings, then Privacy, and turn on Require Unlock.

Ask Again sets how long you can be away before the app asks you to authenticate. We'll keep five minutes here.

The switches below control details outside the app. Each is separate from the lock. Here we're hiding Live Activity details so they stay generic even after the phone is unlocked.

Notifications and Lock Screen widgets also depend on your iPhone settings. Check the written guide for those steps. These choices stay on this device.
