Open Settings, then Siri and Shortcuts. With Plus, you can turn on Allow Siri roster answers here.

The examples on this screen are a place to start. Siri reads the shifts or visits you've saved, so check the plan is up to date first.

For visits, client names stay off unless you choose to say them. Addresses, phone numbers and notes aren't shared with Siri.

If you ask Siri to record an action, it waits for your confirmation before saving. You can undo from Today. Opening Today, Calendar or Import is free.
