Open Settings, then Travel. Your travel buffer is the extra time you want around a journey.

Tap Start Location to set a start address or area, then save it. Check that it's the place you'll actually be leaving from.

Apple Maps estimates are optional. If you turn them on, addresses are sent to Apple for the estimate, not your private notes. Without an estimate, the app uses the buffer.

A leave-by time is a planning aid, not a promise that you'll arrive on time. Check the journey and allow for anything the estimate won't know about.
