Say you're off sick today. You can record that here without changing your rotation.

Find your shift on Today and tap Not working today. We'll choose Sick day here, then tap Save.

There's the absence at the top. It won't count as a shift you've worked. Your future shifts and Calendar events stay as they were.

If you need to let your workplace know, do that separately. This only records the absence in RosterEase.

If you picked the wrong reason, tap Undo beside the record to go back to what was there before.
