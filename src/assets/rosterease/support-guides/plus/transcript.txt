Open Settings, then RosterEase Plus, to check your access and see the plans.

Shifts is free for one workplace. Visits is free for five active clients. Notes, manual check-ins, widgets and backups don't need a subscription.

Finishing your first setup starts a free week of full Plus. That week doesn't charge you or renew by itself. A subscription is a separate choice, and it renews until you cancel.

Use See Plus Plans to compare what's included. Check the App Store price before subscribing. Restore purchases is for a subscription you already have.

You don't need to buy anything to close this screen. If Plus ends, the things you've saved stay.
