For a one-off change, open Calendar and select the date you need. Open the shift editor for that day. We'll choose Off here, then save the change.

That changes this day without rewriting the rotation for future weeks. Check the saved day against what you meant to change.

If it should follow the pattern again, open the editor and remove the change. To change the repeating rotation itself, use Workplaces instead.

And if you're recording that you didn't work a planned shift, use Not working today on Today.
